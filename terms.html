Description of Service

  • The App records work shifts - entered with a live timer or added manually - and calculates earnings from the hourly rate, break deductions, and any overtime rule you configure.
  • If you set rounding for a job, the App rounds the time worked on a timer shift to the nearest block you chose (up or down) when you stop the timer, and stores the adjusted end time. Shifts entered or edited by hand are not rounded.
  • The App can generate PDF timesheets and invoices from the shifts you have recorded, and hand them to your device's share sheet so you can send or save them.
  • The App runs entirely on your device. It requires no account and no internet connection to record shifts or calculate earnings.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Introduction

Generated Media, Inc. ("we", "our", "us") operates Time Clock: Hours Tracker (the "App"), an app for tracking work shifts and calculating earnings, available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the App and who receives it.

In short: everything you enter into the App - your shifts, job sites, hourly rates, earnings, and business details - is stored only on your device, and we receive none of it. The App does contain third-party software development kits (SDKs) from Google and Meta. They collect information about how the App is used, about Pro purchases, and about your device - including device and advertising identifiers and an approximate location derived from your IP address - for analytics, for measuring and running our advertising, for fraud prevention and security, and to show ads. When this starts and what you can control differs between iPhone and Android, as described below.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following in a local database on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • Shifts - start and end times, break duration, the rate and currency applied, overtime rule, and any note you add.
  • Jobs and clients - name, hourly rate, currency, colour, overtime rule, and rounding settings.
  • Business profile - the business name and contact details you optionally add so they appear on exported documents, and your default rate, currency, and overtime rule.
  • App settings - preferences such as the first day of the week and the time format.

This data lives in the App's private storage area on your device. It may be included in your device backups - iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup on iPhone, and Android Backup to your Google Account on Android, if you have these enabled. That is a function of your device's operating system, not of the App, and we have no access to those backups. Deleting the App deletes this data from the device.

Information Collected Through the App

The SDKs listed under "Third-Party Services" collect the following categories of information. The App does not pass them the shifts, rates, earnings, client names, notes, business details, or documents you enter or create.

  • App interactions - events such as the first launch, onboarding finished, a shift started or finished, a shift added, edited, or deleted, a job created, edited, or deleted, the reports screen opened, a PDF exported, the Pro screen shown or closed, a free-tier limit reached, and the result of the tracking permission check. Firebase Analytics also records standard events on its own, such as app opens, sessions, engagement time, and in-app purchases. Events the App sends carry only coarse details: the length of a finished shift in minutes, how many jobs you have or how many shifts are in an exported report, whether a job has a rate or an overtime rule set (yes or no, never the amount), the export type, which screen or limit opened the Pro offer, and the platform (iOS or Android).
  • Purchase history - that a Pro purchase was started, completed, failed, or restored, the product identifier of the plan, and, for a completed purchase, its price and currency.
  • Device and other identifiers - an app installation identifier assigned by Firebase, identifiers the Meta SDK assigns to the installation, and your device's advertising identifier: the Android Advertising ID on Android, and on iPhone the IDFA only if you allow tracking.
  • Approximate location - a coarse location (such as country or city) that the SDKs may derive from your IP address. The App does not request location permission and never accesses GPS or precise location.
  • Advertising data - whether an ad was requested, shown, or tapped.
  • Diagnostics and crash data - technical information the SDKs collect automatically, such as device model, operating system and App version, launch time and other performance data, and, where an SDK reports them, crash or error logs.

We use this information to understand how the App is used and improve it, to measure which advertising campaigns bring people to the App and to optimise those campaigns, to show ads in the free version, and - through Google and Meta - to prevent fraud and keep their services secure. We access analytics only as reports in the Firebase and Meta dashboards; we do not use the information to identify you by name.

When Collection Starts: iPhone and Android

iPhone

  • When the App starts, it shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The App sends no events to Firebase Analytics or Meta App Events and loads no ads before you answer it. The Google ads SDK may already start in the background and check with Google whether a consent form is needed in your region.
  • After you answer - whichever answer you give - Firebase Analytics is switched on and receives the app interactions, purchase information, and diagnostics described above, together with its installation identifier. If you decline tracking, it does not receive your advertising identifier (IDFA), but the events it collects are still sent to Google and processed under Google's policies.
  • Meta App Events is used only if you choose "Allow". In that case it receives the same events and purchase information together with your IDFA. If you decline, the App sends no events, purchases, or advertising identifier to Meta, and the Meta SDK's automatic event logging stays off; the SDK remains in the App and may still contact Meta to load its configuration.
  • Ads are personalised only if you choose "Allow"; otherwise you see non-personalised ads.

Android

  • Android has no tracking prompt. Firebase Analytics and Meta App Events are active from the first launch and receive the app interactions, purchase information, identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), and diagnostics described above. Meta also records App install and activation events automatically, and the SDKs read the Google Play install referrer to learn which ad or store page led to the install.
  • On devices that support it, the Google advertising and measurement SDKs may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox features (Topics, Attribution Reporting, and Protected Audience), which you can manage in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • The App has no in-app switch to turn this collection off. Deleting or resetting your advertising ID in Android settings stops it from being collected or links it to a new value, but events continue to be sent with the installation identifiers. Uninstalling the App stops all collection.

On both platforms, users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other regions where Google requires it are shown Google's consent form (UMP) before ads are requested. That form governs advertising only; it does not control Firebase Analytics or Meta App Events.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) - analytics. firebase.google.com/support/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Meta App Events (Meta Platforms, Inc. and Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) - analytics and measurement of our advertising on Meta's platforms. facebook.com/privacy/policy.
  • Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (Google LLC) - advertising and ad consent. policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Apple App Store (iPhone) and Google Play Billing (Android) - purchases.
  • Apple App Store rating prompt (iPhone) and Google Play In-App Review (Android) - the system prompt the App may occasionally show to ask you to rate it. Any rating or review you leave is submitted to Apple or Google under your store account; we do not receive it through the App.

Google and Meta also process the information they collect under their own privacy policies, linked above.

Advertising

Users who have not purchased Pro may see occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads served by Google AdMob. Purchasing any Pro plan removes ads.

  • To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (and a coarse location derived from it), device type and operating system version, and ad-interaction data.
  • No data you enter into the App is shared with Google, Meta, or any advertiser.
  • On iPhone, ads are personalised only if you allow tracking; otherwise they are non-personalised. On Android, personalisation follows your Android ad settings and, where shown, your answer on Google's consent form.
  • If you decline on Google's consent form, Google serves only limited or non-personalised ads, or none, according to your choices.
  • Purchasing Pro stops ads from being shown, but the advertising SDK remains part of the App.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store on iPhone, and by Google through Google Play on Android. The App receives from the store the list of plans with their prices and whether your Pro entitlement is active. We never see or receive your name, payment card, billing address, Apple Account, or Google Account. As described above, the plan's product identifier, price, and currency are sent to analytics when a purchase completes.

PDF Export and Sharing

PDF invoices and timesheets are generated on your device from data already stored there. When you export a document, it is written to the App's temporary storage on your device and handed to your device's standard share sheet, where you choose what happens to it - email, messaging, saving to a file, and so on. The App does not upload exported documents anywhere, and we never receive a copy. Once you send a document to a third-party app or service, that recipient's own privacy policy applies.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require an account, login, email address, or phone number - the App has no sign-up of any kind.
  • We operate no servers or backend of our own. Your shift, earnings, and client data is never uploaded.
  • We do not collect your name, the content you enter, or the documents you create.
  • We do not collect precise location. The App requests no location permission.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, calendar, or health data. The App requests none of these permissions.
  • We do not collect biometric data of any kind.
  • We do not have access to your payment information.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data for money. The information described under "Information Collected Through the App" is collected by, and therefore shared with, Google (Firebase Analytics and AdMob) and Meta (App Events), and Apple or Google receive what is needed to process your purchases. We do not share the data you enter into the App with anyone.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or valid legal process.

Legal Basis for Processing (EEA and UK Users)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, our legal bases are:

  • Consent - for personalised advertising, for Meta App Events and the use of your advertising identifier on iPhone (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), and for ads under Google's consent form. Consent can be withdrawn at any time (see "Your Choices").
  • Legitimate interests - for product analytics, for measuring the effectiveness of our advertising on Android (where no tracking prompt exists), for serving non-personalised ads that fund the free version, and for fraud prevention and security performed by Google and Meta.
  • Performance of a contract - for verifying your Pro entitlement so that paid features are unlocked.

The data controller for the App is Generated Media, Inc. Google and Meta also process the data they collect as controllers under their own policies.

Your Rights (EEA and UK Users)

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, and receive a portable copy of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

All of your App content is stored solely on your device, so you can exercise most of these rights directly: view and edit any shift, job, or profile detail inside the App, delete individual records, or delete the App to erase everything at once. We hold no copy of it.

The analytics and advertising data described above is linked only to installation and advertising identifiers, not to your name or any account, because the App has none. The App does not provide a way to request deletion of this data. To stop further collection, uninstall the App; to break the link to your advertising identifier, reset or delete it in your device settings (see "Your Choices"). For data held by Google and Meta, you can also use their own privacy tools, linked under "Third-Party Services". You may contact us using the details below with any question about your data, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

International Data Transfers

Google and Meta may process the information they collect in the United States and other countries. Such transfers rely on the safeguards these companies use, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Data Retention

Data you enter is retained on your device for as long as you keep it there, and is deleted when you delete the record or uninstall the App. User-level and event-level analytics data in Firebase is kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics settings; aggregated reports that do not identify a device may be kept longer. Data received by Meta and by Google for advertising is retained under their own policies.

Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA gives you the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

The categories of personal information collected in connection with the App are: identifiers (installation and advertising identifiers), commercial information (Pro purchase history), internet or other electronic network activity (app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostics), and approximate geolocation derived from your IP address. They are used for analytics, for measuring and running our advertising, for showing ads, and for fraud prevention and security.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Making identifiers and app activity available to Meta and Google for ad measurement and personalised advertising may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CPRA. On iPhone you can opt out by declining the tracking prompt (or turning off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in iOS Settings): no events are then sent to Meta, your advertising identifier is not shared, and ads are not personalised; Firebase Analytics events without the advertising identifier continue to be sent to Google. On Android the App has no such switch: deleting your advertising ID or opting out of ads personalisation in Android settings limits the use of the advertising identifier, but app events are still sent to Meta and Google; uninstalling the App stops all sharing. Purchasing Pro stops ads from being displayed but does not by itself stop this collection.

No other category of personal information - not your shifts, earnings, client details, or documents - is collected, sold, or shared.

Your Choices

  • Tracking on iPhone - decline the tracking prompt, or turn tracking off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. No events are then sent to Meta and ads are not personalised; Firebase Analytics continues without your advertising identifier.
  • Advertising ID on Android - in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads, depending on the device) you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation. This does not stop app events from being sent.
  • Remove ads - purchase any Pro plan.
  • Change your advertising consent (where Google's consent form is shown) - the App does not yet have a setting for this; reinstalling the App, or on Android clearing its data, shows the consent form again. Both erase the shifts and jobs stored in the App, so export anything you need first.
  • Stop all collection - uninstall the App. There is no in-app setting that turns analytics off.
  • Delete your App data - delete individual shifts and jobs inside the App, or uninstall the App to remove everything stored on your device.

Data Security

Your App data is protected by the security of your own device, including the device passcode and the operating system's app sandboxing, which prevents other apps from reading the App's storage. Information collected by the SDKs is sent to Google and Meta over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

The App is a professional tool intended for users aged 13 and over, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will address it.
